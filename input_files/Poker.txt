exas Hold'em Poker Rules (No-Limit Texas Hold'em)

Texas Hold'em is the most popular form of poker. The goal is to make the best five-card poker hand using any combination of your two private cards and the five community cards.

1. Setup
Each player receives 2 private cards (called "hole cards").
Five community cards are dealt face-up in the center of the table.
Players share the community cards to build their best five-card hand.
2. The Blinds

Before cards are dealt:

The player to the dealer's left posts the small blind.
The next player posts the big blind (usually twice the small blind).

These forced bets start the action.

3. Betting Rounds
Pre-Flop
Everyone receives 2 hole cards.
Action starts with the player left of the big blind.
Players may:
Fold: discard their hand and leave the round.
Call: match the current bet.
Raise: increase the bet.
The Flop
Three community cards are dealt face-up.
A new betting round begins, starting with the first active player left of the dealer.
The Turn
A fourth community card is dealt.
Another betting round occurs.
The River
A fifth and final community card is dealt.
The last betting round occurs.
Showdown
If two or more players remain after the river betting round:
Players reveal their cards.
The best five-card hand wins the pot.

4. Hand Rankings (Highest to Lowest)
Rank	Hand	Example
1	Royal Flush	A♠ K♠ Q♠ J♠ 10♠
2	Straight Flush	9♥ 8♥ 7♥ 6♥ 5♥
3	Four of a Kind	K♣ K♦ K♥ K♠ 3♣
4	Full House	A♠ A♥ A♦ 7♣ 7♦
5	Flush	Any 5 cards of same suit
6	Straight	Five consecutive ranks
7	Three of a Kind	Q♣ Q♦ Q♥ 8♠ 2♣
8	Two Pair	J♠ J♦ 4♣ 4♥ A♠
9	One Pair	10♠ 10♦ K♣ 7♥ 3♠
10	High Card	A♠ J♦ 9♣ 5♥ 2♠
5. Using Your Cards

You may use:

Both hole cards + 3 community cards
One hole card + 4 community cards
No hole cards at all (play the board)

Your hand is always the best five-card combination available.

Example

Your cards:

A♠ K♠

Board:

Q♠ J♠ 10♠ 2♦ 7♣

Best hand:

A♠ K♠ Q♠ J♠ 10♠
Royal Flush
6. Winning the Pot

You can win by:

Everyone else folds before showdown.
Having the best hand at showdown.
7. Common Terms
Check: Pass action without betting (only if no bet is facing you).
Bet: Put chips into the pot.
Call: Match a bet.
Raise: Increase a bet.
Fold: Discard your hand.
All-In: Bet all your remaining chips.
